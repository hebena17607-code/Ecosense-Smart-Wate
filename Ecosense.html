The problem

Fixed schedules collect from empty bins and miss full ones.

Overflow goes unseen

Nobody knows a bin is full until residents complain. Litter spreads, smells build up and strays and pests arrive.

Wasted trips

Trucks follow the same loop every day. Half-empty stops burn fuel, driver hours and emissions for no benefit.

No data to plan with

Without fill history, councils cannot place bins well, size fleets properly or prove that cleanliness targets are met.

The solution

From sensor to street in four steps.

1

Sense

A lid-mounted ultrasonic or time-of-flight sensor measures the distance to the waste. Tilt, temperature and gas sensors watch for tipping, fire and odour.

2

Transmit

A low-power microcontroller wakes at intervals and sends readings over LoRaWAN, NB-IoT or GSM. A solar cell and battery keep it running for months.

3

Predict

The cloud learns each bin's fill pattern by hour, day and event. It forecasts when every bin will hit the threshold and raises an alert ahead of time.

4

Dispatch

A route optimiser builds a daily plan with only the urgent bins and pushes it to the driver's phone. The dashboard confirms each pickup.

Innovations

More than a full-bin alarm.

Predictive overflow AI

Learns daily and weekly fill curves, plus festivals and market days, so the alert arrives hours before the bin is full.

Dynamic route optimisation

Rebuilds the truck route each morning and whenever a new alert arrives, skipping bins that can safely wait.

Fire, odour and tilt safety

Temperature and gas sensors catch smouldering waste early. A tilt sensor reports bins knocked over or stolen.

Citizen app with QR report

Residents scan a QR code on any bin to report damage or overflow with a photo, then follow the ticket until it is closed.

Smart segregation

An optional camera and on-device vision model spots wet, dry and recyclable waste. It gives instant feedback at the bin and tracks purity per ward.

City-wide analytics

Heatmaps show hotspots, under-served wards and bins that need relocating, giving planners evidence for every decision.

Technology stack

Device
ESP32, ultrasonic or ToF sensor, tilt, temperature and gas sensors, solar cell and Li-ion battery
Network
LoRaWAN, NB-IoT or GSM, with MQTT messaging to the cloud
Cloud and AI
Time-series database, fill-forecast model, vehicle routing solver
Apps
Admin web dashboard, driver mobile app, citizen app with QR reporting

Rollout roadmap

Phase 1 · Pilot
Instrument 50 bins in one ward and calibrate sensors and alert thresholds.
Phase 2 · Optimise
Train the fill-forecast model and switch trucks to dynamic routes.
Phase 3 · Engage
Launch the citizen app and add camera-based segregation to selected bins.
Phase 4 · Scale
Extend city-wide with analytics for planning, tenders and reporting.
